    About the matching

    A small open model compares the meaning of your description with eight park descriptions. It chooses from checked information; it does not write new park facts or routes.

    Matching happens in this browser. Your description is not sent to a model service or stored by this app. First use downloads about 50 MB of public model and runtime files from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. Those hosts receive normal download requests. Later matching uses the loaded model.

    Park information is linked to City of Mississauga and Credit Valley Conservation sources. Check their current access notices and conditions before visiting. No particular route is rated for accessibility.
